# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Keep feedback specific, constructive,
and relevant. Welcome questions and differing experience levels. Do not harass,
threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, disclose private information, or
post unwanted promotional content.

These expectations apply to project-managed spaces, including issues and PRs.
Maintainers may remove harmful content, lock threads, or restrict participation.
Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and context.

For sensitive concerns, request a private contact route from a maintainer
without publishing personal details or allegations. No dedicated confidential
reporting address is currently designated. GitHub's reporting tools are also
available for platform abuse.
